Is the model guessing?

I ask a model the same question ten times. If the answers agree, it probably knows. If they scatter, it is probably guessing. The hard part is deciding which answers mean the same thing. Here Jev (or an NLI model) judges every pair, and the score keeps its probabilities instead of forcing a yes or no.

    How it works

    Sample

    For each question I take one greedy answer, which is the one that gets graded, and ten more answers at temperature 1.

    Compare

    For every pair of the ten answers, Jev (or a DeBERTa NLI model) gives the probability that they mean the same thing.

    Score

    Soft score: how much of the model's probability mass agrees with each answer, averaged. Hard score: cut at 0.5, cluster, take the entropy of cluster sizes.

    mi = meanj P(i,j)  ·  H = −meani log mi
